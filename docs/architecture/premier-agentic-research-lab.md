# Premier Agentic Trading Research Lab

## Category Wedge

Trading Lab should own the category of agentic trading claim disproof. It is
not an AI hedge fund, broker terminal, signal service, or investment advisor.
Its promise is narrower and stronger: bring a trading claim, preserve the
pre-registered evidence, and let specialized research agents try to kill the
claim before anyone trusts it.

The lab wins by being harder to fool than competing agent demos.

## Operating Roadmap

1. Category language: every product surface should say research, falsification,
   evidence, and promotion gates before performance.
2. Visible agents: represent agentic work through bounded research roles such
   as leak auditor, baseline challenger, cost-stress critic, reproducibility
   clerk, regime skeptic, and promotion gatekeeper.
3. Evidence rigor: move beyond column-name leakage checks into point-in-time
   field contracts, unknown-lineage gaps, baseline packs, randomized controls,
   and out-of-sample pressure.
4. Readiness validation: separate artifact presence from promotion readiness.
   Tiny or placeholder paper, drift, calibration, and risk packets must not
   unlock confident setup language.
5. Proof surfaces: expose claim cards, evidence passports, run replay, failed
   strategy galleries, and raw JSON ledgers as views over durable artifacts.
6. Product story: use the API-backed web shell as the demo surface, static
   dashboards as audit artifacts, and snapshot pages only when they match the
   latest observed provenance.
7. CI and deployment hygiene: add automated tests before public sharing, then
   track local, committed, pushed, and deployed/live states explicitly.

## Premier Bar

Premier means the lab can answer these questions with artifacts:

- What was known at the time of the decision?
- Which agent challenged the claim, and what did it find?
- Which baselines and controls did the claim fail or survive?
- How did costs, slippage, liquidity, delay, and regime affect the result?
- What would change our mind?
- Why is this not ready for a live signal, paper review, or public claim?

## Non-Negotiables

- No broker, account, credential, order, or personalized advice surface.
- No Vanta blending without explicit approval.
- JSON evidence remains the source of truth; reports and dashboards are views.
- Failed claims are first-class assets.
- Promotion language is earned by validated evidence, not confident copy.
